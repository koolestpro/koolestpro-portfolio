Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Koolestpro ("we", "us") provides Shopify CRO, design, and content-creation services. This policy covers the tools and apps we operate, including our TikTok content publishing tool.

What we collect

Our TikTok publishing tool only accesses the TikTok account it is explicitly authorized against by its owner. It uses this access solely to upload video content to that account's TikTok inbox as private drafts for manual review and publishing by the account owner. We do not collect, store, or process data about any other TikTok user, follower, or viewer.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share any account data with third parties.
  • We do not use TikTok data for advertising or analytics unrelated to our own content operations.
  • We do not post or publish content automatically without a human reviewing and approving it first.

Data retention

Access tokens are stored only as long as needed to operate the publishing tool and are not shared outside Koolestpro. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account's connected-apps settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach out via koolestpro-portfolio.vercel.app.
